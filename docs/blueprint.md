# **App Name**: AMEC Billing Hub

## Core Features:

- Role-Based Authentication: Secure login system allowing separate access and functionalities for Administrators and Salespersons.
- Customer Relationship Management: A centralized system for administrators and salespersons to create, view, update, and manage customer records.
- Product & Service Catalog: Admins can manage a catalog of products and services, including descriptions, pricing, and availability.
- Invoice Creation & Editing: Salespersons can generate new invoices using customer and product data, while Administrators have full editing capabilities.
- Invoice Status Tracking: Functionality to track the status of invoices (e.g., pending, paid, overdue) for better financial oversight.
- AI-Powered Sales Summary Tool: For administrators, a generative AI tool to provide quick, concise summaries and key insights from sales data.
- Role-Specific Dashboards: Tailored dashboards displaying relevant metrics and quick actions for both Administrators and Salespersons upon login.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated deep blue-green (#2988A3), conveying professionalism and reliability. It ensures clear contrast in a light scheme.
- Background color: A very light, subtle blue-grey (#F2F7F9) for a clean, open, and professional workspace.
- Accent color: A vibrant electric blue-violet (#4A57E9), chosen for its dynamic contrast to highlight calls to action and important interactive elements.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, highly legible, and neutral design, ensuring clarity across all data and textual content.
- Utilize clean, modern, and functional line icons or glyphs to maintain a professional and uncluttered aesthetic throughout the system.
- Implement organized, grid-based, and responsive layouts that adapt seamlessly to various screen sizes, ensuring easy readability of data tables and dashboards.
- Incorporate subtle and functional animations for transitions, data updates, and user feedback, enhancing the overall user experience without distraction.
